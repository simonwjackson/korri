# Shift part-first atomic conversion roadmap

Roadmap to realize the "parts are the application, devices are mounts" vision in the
theme-workshop dev-lab, scoped to the **Shift** surface. Makes axes/inputs/events
uniform part-level edges, mounts placed parts through the same real registry path as
live devices (so device facts like battery/network reach parts through their real
production derivation), and decomposes Shift's remaining monolithic surfaces (Detail,
Library) into pure atomic-design parts. pico/boxbuster and the multi-surface rollout
are deferred to a separate future roadmap.
