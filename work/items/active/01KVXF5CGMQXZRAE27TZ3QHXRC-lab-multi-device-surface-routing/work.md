# URL-addressable multi-device Shift lab

Turn the design-tool lab into a place where you click through the REAL Shift application at several physical device sizes simultaneously. Each visible frame mounts the real surface via `mountShift`, all driven by one canonical surface route, so navigation behaves like the real app instead of jumping between fixture pages. Device set, theme, and surface route all become URL-addressable, synced across frames, with no page reload.
