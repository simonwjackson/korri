# New-release wall — on a cadence

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth.

**Location:** the front endcap by the entrance.

**Return ritual:** the wall restocks on a real rhythm (a weekly "new arrivals" drop), and last
week's front-wall games migrate deeper to their permanent genre home. You start to know roughly
*when* things appear and *where* they go.

**Placeness:** the Hacker News "recognise the rhythms" kind of placeness — temporal cadence gives
you a reason to return on a schedule, and the consistent migration path teaches the store's
geography (**stable layout, prop 1** + **edges, prop 3**: a bounded, knowable rotation, not an
infinite feed).

**Notes:** "new releases" can map to newly-installed games, recently-acquired ROMs, or an editorial
spotlight. Connects to survivor #1 (self-merchandising store) in the parent ideation doc.
