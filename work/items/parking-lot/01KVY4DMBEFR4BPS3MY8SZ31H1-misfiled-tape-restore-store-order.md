# The misfiled tape — restore the store's order

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth.

**Location:** anywhere on the shelves.

**Return ritual:** now and then a game is in the wrong aisle — left there by you last time, or by a
phantom previous customer. Reshelving it correctly is a small act of care the store remembers; do it
enough and it feeds the trust that earns the back room and the keys.

**Placeness:** the subtle one — it means the store has a *correct state* that can be disturbed and
restored. **Somewhere you can tidy is somewhere real**: noise can't be misfiled, only a place with
order can. Reinforces **stable layout (prop 1)** as something actively maintained, partly by you.

[[returns-chute-refiled-by-staff]] (staff maintain order; you can too).
