# Your hold shelf — a corner that is yours

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth.

**Location:** behind the counter — a "HELD FOR: [you]" shelf.

**Return ritual:** games you're curious about but don't play get a handwritten card and sit on
*your* shelf across visits. It fills up over time, tells the story of your intentions, and the
clerk ribs you when it gets too tall.

**Placeness:** the strongest form of **affordance tied to location (prop 4)** — a specific spot in
the store that belongs to you and that you return to and build a relationship with. It's the
diegetic, spatial form of a wishlist/bookmark, kept content-first (objects on a shelf, not a list).

**Notes:** ties to the clerk-relationship arc [[regular-progression-customer-to-keyholder]]. A full
hold shelf is also a soft prompt to actually play something.
