# Closing time — the store has a nightlife

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth. From the first
> meta-experience batch; user liked it. Absorbs the "lights out" idea (see Notes).

**Location:** the whole floor — triggered by lingering, or the light switch by the door.

**Ritual / state:** the store *closes*: overhead fluorescents drop to security-red, the shutter
comes halfway down, a janitor wheels out a mop bucket — and the games start to stir. Covers whisper,
a sprite peeks out of a box, a tiny character climbs onto a shelf edge (Toy-Story-when-the-lights-go-
out). Step toward the door and it snaps back.

**Placeness:** a bounded **time-of-day state (edges, prop 3)** and a distinct **location-bound mode
(prop 4)** — the store affords different things after hours. A reason to linger past the task.

**Notes — absorbs "lights out":** the original separate "power outage + flashlight" idea was
incoherent (the demo TVs would still glow during an outage). Resolved by folding it in here: it's not
an outage, it's *closing* dimming the floor while the display CRTs stay on their own always-on circuit.
The "haunted tapes that glow only in the dark / a hidden catalog visible after-hours" hook lives in
this closing-time mode.
