# Dignified at N=5, navigable at N=50,000 — on a handheld and a TV

> Split from the blockbuster-ps1 ideation (idea #5). Full grounding context lives in the parent
> ideation doc: `work/items/parking-lot/01KVX589XXVGE3MM0G5GHY042Y-blockbuster-ps1-launcher-browsing-ux.md`.

**Description:** Three fixed aisles are charming at 30 games, embarrassing at 5, impossible at 5,000.
Make layout scale-adaptive: at tiny N, museum-style spotlit pedestals (ceremony, not aisles); at huge
N, the far field becomes a non-itemized "spine-wall" mosaic you navigate by colored strata/era/genre,
resolving to individual titles only on approach. Drive the same scene across devices via intrinsic-design
density-over-zoom: walk-to-browse on a handheld, gaze/spotlight-sweep from the couch on a TV.

**Warrant:** `direct:` fixed "three gondola aisles" vs a NES→modern catalog + Korri's intrinsic design
(density-over-zoom, handheld→TV). `external:` LaunchBox lags past ~1000 covers and Cover Flow died at
scale — honest large-library nav must aggregate before it itemizes.

**Rationale:** Scale and device-range are existential for a launcher; a metaphor that works at only one
library size and one screen size is a screenshot, not a launcher.

**Downsides:** Spine-wall LOD is real rendering work; the empty/near-empty first-run state needs as much
design love as the power-user state (usually skipped).
