# The members-only back room

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth. From the first
> meta-experience batch; user approved.

**Location:** a curtained **STAFF ONLY** doorway → the back room, lit by a single buzzing bulb.

**Return ritual:** the clerk slowly clocks you as a regular. Somewhere there's a membership card to
find/earn; flash it at the curtain and the back room opens — the cult stuff, the bootlegs, the hidden
gems that aren't on the main floor.

**Placeness:** a bounded, gated **location with its own identity and affordances (props 3 & 4)** — a
distinct room, not a filter. Earned access makes it the payoff of the
[[regular-progression-customer-to-keyholder]] arc.

**Notes:** the back room's catalog could be the "deep cuts" / weird-and-wonderful slice — a curatorial
opportunity. Reward for return, not a thing you can rush.
