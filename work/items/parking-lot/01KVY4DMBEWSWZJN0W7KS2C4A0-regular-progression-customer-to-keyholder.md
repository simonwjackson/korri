# Customer → regular → keyholder

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. This is the spine the other
> rituals hang off. Optional depth — the fast path never requires it.

**Location:** the counter, then eventually the whole store.

**Return ritual:** visits accumulate a quiet trace — a punch card, a polaroid pinned up after your
50th night, the clerk's greeting shifting from neutral to familiar. Far enough in, you're trusted
with the back room (see [[members-only-back-room]]); at the very end you're handed the **keys to
lock up**: kill the lights, lower the shutter, set the alarm, alone.

**Placeness:** the literal embodiment of "placeness can be designed *for* but not *in* — it develops
through return, ritual, and use." Being trusted to close the place is the ultimate "I belong here."

**Notes:** the meta-story's progression backbone — most other meta-experiences feed trust into this
arc (reshelving misfiled tapes, returning tapes, being a regular). Depends on session persistence /
the deferred go-home loop as its clock.
