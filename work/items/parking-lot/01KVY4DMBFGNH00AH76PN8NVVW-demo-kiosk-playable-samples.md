# The demo kiosk — playable samples + scan-to-preview

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth. From the first
> meta-experience batch; user approved with a "yes, and".

**Location:** a standing kiosk in the corner running an attract loop.

**Ritual:** walk up, press it, and get a ~20-second *playable* sliver of a game right there in PS1
chunk — a single jump, a single fight, a single hole of golf. Browsing becomes sampling.

**Yes-and (user):** "scanning" the tape you're holding shows a short **video snippet** (e.g. a YouTube
clip) of the game — a held-object preview that complements the kiosk's hands-on sample. Two preview
modes: *watch* (scan in hand) and *play* (at the kiosk).

**Placeness:** **affordance tied to location (prop 4)** — sampling lives at the kiosk and in your
hands, not in a menu. The kiosk is its own little arcade within the store.

**Notes:** the held-tape scan overlaps with survivor #2 (zone-the-diegesis) in the parent ideation
doc — the video snippet wants a stabilized, legible surface, not the dithered world. Streaming video
in-world is a real implementation cost to flag.
