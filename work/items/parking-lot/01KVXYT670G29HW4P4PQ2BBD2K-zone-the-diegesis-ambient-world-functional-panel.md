# Zone the diegesis — ambient PS1 world, stabilized functional panel

> Split from the blockbuster-ps1 ideation (idea #2). Full grounding context lives in the parent
> ideation doc: `work/items/parking-lot/01KVX589XXVGE3MM0G5GHY042Y-blockbuster-ps1-launcher-browsing-ux.md`.

**Description:** Draw an explicit line where the lo-fi look stops paying off. The walkable PS1 store
stays a gorgeous *ambient set*; the decision-critical work — reading a blurb, confirming a pick —
happens on a stabilized, dither-suppressed, high-contrast diegetic surface (a rental-counter screen,
or the held box's "focus-read" inset), rendered from the same library state. That same panel doubles
as the reduced-motion / high-contrast **parity view**, so motion-sensitive or controller-only users
get a first-class path, never a degraded one. The store is never the *only* way in.

**Warrant:** `external:` VR media rooms keep the actual picker as a flat panel — a documented signal of
where diegesis stops helping; Cover Flow died partly from being animation-heavy at scale. `direct:`
rough edges "PS1 dithering hurts text legibility" + "MOUSE-ONLY nav" + the grounding's explicit
"reduced-motion high-contrast fallback" requirement.

**Rationale:** Resolves three separate blockers — legibility, accessibility, and the historical failure
mode of cover-first nav — with one decision: one state, two renderings.

**Downsides:** Risks feeling like "two UIs"; the ambient/functional line must be drawn carefully or the
magic leaks out (over-flattened VR rooms lose their point).

**Confidence:** 82%
**Complexity:** Medium
**Status:** Unexplored
