# Redesign the launch moment — collapse the trip, keep the commit

> Split from the blockbuster-ps1 ideation (idea #4). Full grounding context lives in the parent
> ideation doc: `work/items/parking-lot/01KVX589XXVGE3MM0G5GHY042Y-blockbuster-ps1-launcher-browsing-ux.md`.

**Description:** The carry-to-console-in-another-room trip is the most spatially expensive step and ends
in a bare cover-on-TV with no metadata. Two opposing fixes are on the table: **(a) collapse** — the held
box itself powers on as a live diorama preview, and a single "declare/confirm" beat *is* the launch (no
second room); or **(b) enrich** — keep the room but make the TV the moment real metadata arrives (resume
point, last-played, controller hints, confirm-to-launch). Either way the highest-intent moment should
carry the most information and a deliberate commit.

**Warrant:** `direct:` rough edge "bare console load (no metadata, no real launch intent)." `external:`
customs "declare what you're carrying" supplies a ceremonial single-item commit; VR rooms suggest the
long carry-trip is a low-value diegetic step.

**Rationale:** The payoff of the entire browse is currently empty; this is where "browse" becomes
"play" and it deserves to be the most considered beat.

**Downsides:** Collapsing discards a bit of theater you just built (the viewing room); enriching keeps
the walk-tax. A genuine fork.
