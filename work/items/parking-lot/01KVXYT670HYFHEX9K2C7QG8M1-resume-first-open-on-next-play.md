# Resume-first — open on the one obvious next play

> Split from the blockbuster-ps1 ideation (idea #7). Full grounding context lives in the parent
> ideation doc: `work/items/parking-lot/01KVX589XXVGE3MM0G5GHY042Y-blockbuster-ps1-launcher-browsing-ux.md`.

**Description:** Most launcher sessions are "get me back into the thing I was playing," yet the spatial
fantasy currently taxes that with a walk. Open the experience *already focused on* your continue-playing
tape (the "tape still in the deck" / a single lit box in an after-hours store), with one action to
resume. Browsing the aisles becomes a deliberate opt-in for when you actually want to discover — not a
toll on every launch.

**Warrant:** `direct:` rough edges "no continue-playing" + "bare console load." `external:` Cover Flow
abandoned for being slow at scale (delight must be skippable). `reasoned:` on a handheld you open the
launcher to play, not to tour.

**Rationale:** Protects the highest-frequency real use case so the art-forward browse stays a joy you
*choose*, not a chore you endure — the guardrail that keeps the concept usable day-to-day.

**Downsides:** Lean too hard and the beautiful store rarely gets seen (undercuts the whole reason to
build it); needs a graceful "nothing in progress yet" first-run variant.

**Confidence:** 80%
**Complexity:** Low-Medium
**Status:** Unexplored
