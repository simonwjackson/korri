# The returns chute — post a tape, the staff refile it

> Part of the blockbuster-ps1 "meta-story / placeness" layer. Optional depth.

**Location:** a returns slot by the front door.

**Return ritual:** when you're done with a game you post it through the chute instead of dropping
it. Next visit, the staff have refiled it — back in its home spot, spine-out, a touch more worn. A
tape you *don't* return is still on the floor where you left it.

**Placeness:** two properties at once — the act of returning is a **ritual** (you came back and the
place kept working without you), and the refiling proves the store has a **correct order that
persists (prop 1)**. This is also the clean answer to "rewind for media with no narrative": a tape's
state is *how you left the object* (returned & reshelved vs. left out), not story position.

**Notes:** pairs with [[be-kind-rewind-object-state]] and [[misfiled-tape-restore-store-order]] —
all three treat the tape as an object with a maintained state. Depends on session persistence.
