2026-06-01: Researched Sunshine/Moonlight/GameStream runtime resolution switching. Evidence points to negotiated-at-session-start resolution plus host-display prep/start scripts; true in-session resolution renegotiation is not documented as supported. User reports around freezes after resolution changes exist, but most public workarounds restart the stream/session, use pre-start resolution automation, virtual display/EDID, or align host and stream resolution before launch.
